# H440 B3 CFRP machining notes

All DXFs are 1:1 in millimetres. Use the matching STEP for thickness and feature checks. Cut C01–C04 from 2 mm laminate, one each; C05/C06 from 4 mm laminate, one each; C07 from 4 mm laminate, quantity four. Specify solid CFRP with 0/90 and ±45 reinforcement and obtain the supplier's material certificate.

The B3 center side plates include four through-bolt holes. Keep the widened electronics-deck tongue shoulders, the extended battery-deck camera tabs and the front-center camera clearance cutout. Manufacture the four B3 center plates as a matching set.

Nominal tongues are 2×12 mm; mortises are 2.2×12.2 mm with R0.6 corner relief for cutters no larger than 1.2 mm. Fit to actual laminate thickness and cutter capability using a trial coupon. Do not scale all holes or slots. Through-holes only; no countersinking. Deburr without rounding away structural shoulders. Check closed profiles, tooling access and fit before production.

Purchase/cut four aluminium OD6/ID3.3×62 mm spacers separately. Match their lengths and square their end faces; coordinate the final length with the actual deck-shoulder width. They are not CFRP profiles in this package.
