# H440 B3 printable STEP delivery

The package contains 18 printed-part definitions. Print P06R/P06L camera sides in TPU 95A, W01–W04 wings in PLA, and the remaining P parts in ABS. Follow BOM quantities and optional status. Individual parts fit a 256 mm printer; assembly coordinates are not print orientations.

Camera walls are 5 mm thick and both mounting feet are 3 mm thick. Use eight M3 mounting screws, eight OD5/ID3.2×3 mm metal crush sleeves and broad washers across the two sides. Use dense 95A prints with solid feet/hole regions and at least six perimeter walls. First print fit coupons for the 5.1 mm sleeve holes and M2 slots. Match sleeve length to actual foot thickness to avoid either crushing TPU or leaving it loose.

Orient and support the projecting feet without damaging the camera contact faces or curved slots. Keep load-carrying motor clevises and spar collars in ABS. Do not substitute TPU for these structural fittings. Check M2 screw engagement, the full angle sweep, warm angle retention and vibration after assembly. Dual-ended mounting does not make TPU rigid.
