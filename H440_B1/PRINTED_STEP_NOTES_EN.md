# H440 B1 Printed STEP Delivery Notes

`H440_B1_printed_parts_STEP.zip` contains 19 printable CAD masters: 15 `P*.step` fittings/equipment mounts and four `W*.step` PLA wing sections.

- W01–W04: PLA wing sections.
- P01–P05: ABS angles, spar collars, motor clevises and tail shoes.
- P06L/P06R: DJI O4 Pro camera brackets with an M2 pivot and 0–90° locking slot.
- P07: standard O4 Pro air-unit adapter.
- P08–P14: alternative O4, analog-video, gimbal-interface and generic-camera parts. Select only the parts required by the actual configuration.

STEP is the geometry master. `_print.stl` files provide suggested orientation only. Inspect slicer previews for minimum walls, supports, hole compensation and ABS shrinkage.

The O4 Pro brackets have 20.4 mm nominal internal clearance. Make a fit coupon before printing the complete set. Use thin washers under the M2 slot fasteners, and keep camera-thread engagement within 2 mm.

Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` in the archive to verify transfer integrity.
